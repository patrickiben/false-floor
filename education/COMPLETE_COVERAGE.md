## Equations and inferential conventions

F04 teaches conditional false-eat/yield. F06 distinguishes the old point fraction from its exact lower reference. F10 explains rounding a seed mean into a diagnostic integer. F11 derives the zero-event bound from (1−p)^n; F12 explains the Bonferroni substitution. F44 covers S1’s class laws, likelihood ratio, coincidence, posterior/prior and divergence/information consequences. F45 explains both the mutual-information inequality and constrained Bayes-frontier inequality. F46 teaches conditional auxiliary information and the yield cost of a conjunction rule. The implementation ledger preserves the main model and sampling settings. No formula is treated as evidence that its empirical antecedent or sampling assumptions were independently verified.

## References and administrative matter

The full bibliography is accounted for as the exact source’s reference index. Cited work is explained where it supplies biological context, formal machinery, provenance or comparison practice. This companion does not pretend to teach or independently validate the full contents of every cited paper.
